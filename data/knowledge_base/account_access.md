# Account Access

## Locked accounts
Accounts lock after 5 failed sign-in attempts. Unlock by sending a verification link to the email address on file. Support must never ask for, or accept, a customer's password.

## Password resets
Customers can reset passwords from the sign-in page. Reset links expire after 30 minutes.

## Two-factor authentication
If a customer lost their second factor device, escalate to the identity verification team; do not bypass two-factor on support's authority.
