# Billing and Payments

## Duplicate charges
If a customer was charged twice for one order, verify both charges against the order id. The duplicate charge is refunded automatically within 5 to 7 business days once verified. No return is needed.

## Invoices and receipts
Invoices are available in the order history page as PDFs. Support can email a copy on request after verifying the order id. This is an information request, not a refund request.

## Failed payments
A failed payment is usually a bank authorization problem. Ask the customer to retry with the same card or another payment method. Never ask a customer to send a full card number.
