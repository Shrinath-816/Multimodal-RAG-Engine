# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused and in original packaging unless they arrived damaged or defective.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged, cracked, or defective, the customer is entitled to a refund or a free replacement, their choice. Ask for the order id (format ORD-1234) and a photo of the item and the packaging. Do not require the customer to ship the damaged item back for orders under 100 dollars.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are issued within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds above 100 dollars require supervisor approval before they are issued.

## Non-returnable items
Opened software licenses, gift cards, and personalized items cannot be returned unless they are defective.
