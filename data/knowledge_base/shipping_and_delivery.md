# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery estimates
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Delivery estimates start when the order ships, not when it is placed.

## Late or lost packages
If a package is more than 3 business days late, open a carrier trace using the tracking number. If the carrier cannot locate the package within 5 business days, send a replacement at no cost or refund the order, at the customer's choice.

## Crushed or damaged packages
When the outer box arrives crushed or torn, ask the customer to photograph the box and the shipping label. A replacement ships at no cost; the claim is filed with the carrier by our team, not the customer. Category for these tickets is shipping unless the product itself is also broken.

## Tracking
Tracking links are emailed when the order ships and are available in the order history page.
